Source: Reversim Summit 2026 call-for-papers export, 2026-09-16. All figures are aggregates of anonymized submissions.
  • Counts exclude CFP test entries and same-format duplicate resubmissions. A talk pitched as both a full session and a lightning talk counts as two sessions.
  • Speaker totals count each person once. Track and format rows count speaker slots, so a co-presenter counts once per session.
  • Gender is self-reported where given. Blank profiles were filled in from the speaker's bio, first name or photo.
  • Companies come from speaker taglines and company websites. One-person consultancies are grouped as "Independent".
  • "Mentions AI" means the title or description contains terms such as AI, LLM, agent, MCP, RAG, GenAI or prompt.
  • Tag cloud: English words only, counted once per session, with common filler words removed.
  • "Accepted" is the program committee's decision on a submission. Past participation is matched against the CFP exports of earlier summits — 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025; there is no 2023 export — by email, then by full name, so anyone who changed both is counted as a first-timer.
